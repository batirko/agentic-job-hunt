# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

Your evidence, written once and reused across every process. The evaluation
modes read this to judge Fit, and the interview modes read it to choose which
story leads a given probe.

## How it works

One entry per defensible piece of work. "Defensible" means you can answer three
follow-up questions about it without inventing anything. Five stories you can
defend three levels down beat fifteen you can only open.

Tag each entry with the archetype it serves, so `interview-prep` can map a
question type to a story without re-reading everything.

## Delivery Contract (MANDATORY before using any story)

**The canonical answer structure is the six-beat answer in
[seniority-playbook.md](seniority-playbook.md) section 13.** Read it there.

It is deliberately not restated in this file. Two structures in one corpus is
how a beat goes missing, and the beat that goes missing is almost always the
customer.

Two things to know about stories as written down, in any story bank:

- **Written entries open with the product or the system. Spoken answers must
  not.** The page invites you to set the scene; the interview does not. Beat 2
  has to be rebuilt at delivery time, every time.
- **Written entries compress the decision into activity** ("ran discovery, led
  execution"). That compression hides the one thing being graded. Excavate the
  decision and the option you rejected in advance, using
  [rejected-option-excavation.md](rejected-option-excavation.md).

About 90 seconds, then offer the depth menu. One example per question. Land the
outcome and stop talking; the follow-up question is the goal.

## Stories

> **FILL IN.** Template for each entry:

### [Archetype tag] {Short name} — {One-line framing}

**Situation.** The state of the world, and who it was bad for. A person or a
team, not a system.

**Task.** What you owned, honestly scoped. Name what you did not own.

**Action.** The options that existed, the one you chose, and the one you
rejected. First person singular where it was your call.

**Result.** The number, with an honest attribution caveat if the credit is
shared.

**Reflection.** What is now permanent in how you work because of this. This is
the beat that reads senior; without it the story stops at competent.

**Depth tree.** Three follow-up questions an interviewer would ask, and your
answers. If you cannot fill this in, the story is not ready to lead.
